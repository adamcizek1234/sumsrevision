GEM Revision: how to install and open

1. Drag "GEM Revision" onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open Applications and double-click GEM Revision.
   macOS will say it can't check the app for malicious software. Click Done (or OK).
3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the Security section,
   and click "Open Anyway" next to "GEM Revision". Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

You only need to do this once. After that it opens normally.

Why the warning? The app isn't signed with a paid Apple developer certificate.
It's a simple offline revision app: notes, drugs and quiz, no internet or account needed.
Needs macOS 12 (Monterey) or newer, on any Apple silicon or Intel Mac.
