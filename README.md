# GEM Revision

An offline Mac app for revising the Swansea GEM course, Years 1 and 2.

- **Learning outcomes**: notes for every learning outcome, week by week (62 weeks, 972 LOs). Rate each one *Not yet*, *Shaky* or *Confident*, and edit the notes to make them your own.
- **Anatomy**: notes for 51 anatomy topics from Year 1 and Year 2, with an anatomy quiz that has its own Daily 10 and streak.
- **ICM**: notes for all 441 ICM learning outcomes from Year 1 and Year 2, arranged into a page per topic for each year: body systems such as cardiovascular and respiratory, and core skills such as history taking, examination and prescribing.
- **Drugs**: a reference covering 138 drugs in 12 groups, from heart and blood pressure to fluids and electrolytes, with how each one works, uses, side effects and contraindications.
- **Quiz**: over 4,300 short-answer, key-number and put-in-order questions, plus drug drills. Modes include Daily 10, a 60-second sprint, drilling your shaky LOs, spaced-repetition review and custom quizzes. You can step back through earlier questions in a round, and if it marks you wrong unfairly, tap "I was right" and your answer counts from then on.
- **Progress**: see how far you've got across each year, each week and the drug list, and everything you've mastered.

The app starts empty. Your ratings, quiz history, saved answers and edited notes are saved on your Mac only. It needs no internet connection and no account.

## Download and install

1. Download **[GEM_Revision.dmg](GEM_Revision.dmg)**. On that page, click the download button ("Download raw file").
2. Open the downloaded file and drag **GEM Revision** onto the **Applications** folder.
3. Open Applications and double-click **GEM Revision**. macOS will say it can't check the app for malicious software. Click **Done** (or **OK**).
4. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down to the **Security** section and click **Open Anyway** next to "GEM Revision". Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

You only need to do steps 3 and 4 once. After that, the app opens normally.

macOS shows the warning because the app isn't signed with a paid Apple developer certificate.

**Requirements:** macOS 12 (Monterey) or newer, on an Apple silicon or Intel Mac.

## Notes

A few anatomy learning outcomes don't have notes yet.

This is not an official Swansea University resource. Always check the notes against your course materials and current guidelines.
